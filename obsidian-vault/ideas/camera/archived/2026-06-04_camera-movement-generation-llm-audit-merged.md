# Camera Movement Generation LLM 审查与合并整理

> [!abstract] 结论先行
> 两份 LLM 稿件都抓到了一个真实趋势：2024 之后 camera trajectory generation / camera-controlled video 明显升温。但它们都把“相机轨迹生成”“给定轨迹的视频生成”“普通物体/光流运动控制”“相机姿态估计数据集”混在了一起。  
> 合并后的判断是：**[[analysis/ECCV_2024/E_T_the_Exceptional_Trajectories_Text_to_camera_trajectory_generation_with_character_awareness.md\|E.T.]]、[[analysis/CGF_2024/Cinematographic_Camera_Diffusion_Model.md\|Cinematographic Camera Diffusion]]、[[analysis/NEURIPS_2024/Director3D_Real_world_Camera_Trajectory_and_3D_Scene_Generation_from_Text.md\|Director3D]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2024/ChatCam_Empowering_Camera_Control_through_Conversational_AI.md\|ChatCam]]、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/GenDoP_Auto_regressive_Camera_Trajectory_Generation_as_a_Director_of_Photography.md\|GenDoP/DataDoP]]、[[analysis/ICLR_2026/Pulp_Motion_Framing_aware_multimodal_camera_and_human_motion_generation.md\|Pulp Motion]]、[[analysis/CVPR_2026/Towards_Storytelling_Animations_Joint_Synthesis_of_Human_and_Camera_Motions.md\|Towards Storytelling Animations]]、[[analysis/ICLR_2026/AdaViewPlanner_Adapting_Video_Diffusion_Models_for_Viewpoint_Planning_in_4D_Scenes.md\|AdaViewPlanner]]** 属于或贴近主线；**[[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2024/MotionCtrl_A_Unified_and_Flexible_Motion_Controller_for_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCtrl]]、[[analysis/ICLR_2025/CameraCtrl_Enabling_Camera_Control_for_Text_to_Video_Generation.md\|CameraCtrl]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2025/Free_Form_Motion_Control_A_Synthetic_Video_Generation_Dataset_with_Controllable_Camera_and_Object_Motions.md\|FMC/SynFMC]]、[[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2025/MotionCanvas_Cinematic_Shot_Design_with_Controllable_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCanvas]]、`gpt.md`-I2V、[[analysis/CVPR_2025/GEN3C_3D_Informed_World_Consistent_Video_Generation_with_Precise_Camera_Control.md\|GEN3C]]、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/ReCamMaster_Camera_Controlled_Generative_Rendering_from_A_Single_Video.md\|ReCamMaster]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2025/TrajectoryCrafter_Redirecting_Camera_Trajectory_for_Monocular_Videos_via_Diffusion_Models.md\|TrajectoryCrafter]]、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/Stable_Virtual_Camera_Generative_View_Synthesis_with_Diffusion_Models.md\|Stable Virtual Camera]]** 更适合作为下游 controller / actuator；**SketchVideo、[[analysis/CVPR_2025/MotionPro_A_Precise_Motion_Controller_for_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionPro]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2023/AnimateAnything_Fine_Grained_Open_Domain_Image_Animation_with_Motion_Guidance.md\|AnimateAnything]]、AKiRa** 不应作为 camera movement generation 主线。

审查时点：2026-06-04 Asia/Shanghai。  
审查方式：两个子 agent 并行审查 `gemini.md` 与 `gpt.md`，再用本地 KB、arXiv、CVF/OpenAccess、OpenReview、项目页和已有系统调研笔记交叉核验。DeepSeek 咨询调用未返回可用内容，未作为证据使用。  
相关系统综述见 [[ideas/poool/2026-06-03_camera-movement-generation-survey.md|Camera Movement Generation 系统调研]]。

## 1. 判定口径

本文统一用四层口径，避免把不同任务混在一起：

| 层级                                | 定义                                              |  可收为主线吗 | 例子                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Camera movement generation     | 从文本、动作、音乐、故事、场景语义直接生成 camera pose / trajectory  |       是 | [[analysis/ECCV_2024/E_T_the_Exceptional_Trajectories_Text_to_camera_trajectory_generation_with_character_awareness.md\|E.T.]], [[analysis/ICCV_2025/GenDoP_Auto_regressive_Camera_Trajectory_Generation_as_a_Director_of_Photography.md\|GenDoP]], [[analysis/arxiv_2024/ChatCam_Empowering_Camera_Control_through_Conversational_AI.md\|ChatCam]], [[analysis/ICLR_2026/Pulp_Motion_Framing_aware_multimodal_camera_and_human_motion_generation.md\|Pulp Motion]] |
| B. Camera-controlled video / NVS  | 已给定 camera trajectory，模型负责生成或重渲染视频              |    辅助主线 | [[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2024/MotionCtrl_A_Unified_and_Flexible_Motion_Controller_for_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCtrl]], [[analysis/ICLR_2025/CameraCtrl_Enabling_Camera_Control_for_Text_to_Video_Generation.md\|CameraCtrl]], [[analysis/CVPR_2025/GEN3C_3D_Informed_World_Consistent_Video_Generation_with_Precise_Camera_Control.md\|GEN3C]], [[analysis/ICCV_2025/ReCamMaster_Camera_Controlled_Generative_Rendering_from_A_Single_Video.md\|ReCamMaster]]            |
| C. Sketch / interactive-to-camera | 用户画路径、拖拽相机或在 3D scene-space 指定 camera path      |     半主线 | `gpt.md`-I2V, [[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2025/MotionCanvas_Cinematic_Shot_Design_with_Controllable_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCanvas]], [[analysis/arxiv_2025/Free_Form_Motion_Control_A_Synthetic_Video_Generation_Dataset_with_Controllable_Camera_and_Object_Motions.md\|FMC]]                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| D. 辅助数据 / 评测 / 估计                 | 有 camera pose、camera label 或 SfM/SLAM 估计，但不生成运镜 | 只放数据/工具 | RealEstate10K, DynPose-100K, CameraBench                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

关键边界：

- `2D trajectory -> video motion` 不等于 `2D sketch -> 6-DoF camera trajectory`。
- `camera pose dataset` 不等于 `camera movement generation dataset`，除非带文本/意图/角色/相机轨迹生成任务定义。
- `camera control` 多数是给定轨迹后执行；`camera generation` 要求模型自己从语义或动作中提出轨迹。

两个 agent 的共同结论：

- `gpt.md` 主线方向可用，但可信度中等偏低，错误集中在数字、venue、作者和任务边界。
- `gemini.md` 更像趋势稿，部分最新条目真实，但混有较多 off-topic 和营销化判断。

### 4.3 Sketch / interactive-to-camera：现状仍不成熟

严格定义是：用户画 2D/3D stroke、路径或 storyboard cue，系统输出 camera pose / camera path。按这个定义，现代生成论文很少。

| 类型 | 代表 | 结论 |
|---|---|---|
| 早期 HCI sketch path -> camera navigation | Path Drawing for 3D Walkthrough, Sketch-Based Navigation | 是真正 sketch/path-to-camera，但不是现代 diffusion/video generation |
| 3D scene-space drag/draw camera path | `gemini.md`-I2V, [[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2025/MotionCanvas_Cinematic_Shot_Design_with_Controllable_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCanvas]], [[analysis/arxiv_2025/Free_Form_Motion_Control_A_Synthetic_Video_Generation_Dataset_with_Controllable_Camera_and_Object_Motions.md\|FMC]] | 最接近现代可用方案，但通常是用户已经指定路径，模型负责执行 |
| 2D point / object trajectory video control | [[analysis/ICLR_2026/Pulp_Motion_Framing_aware_multimodal_camera_and_human_motion_generation.md\|Pulp Motion]], `camera generation`, [[analysis/ECCV_2024/DragAnything_Motion_Control_for_Anything_using_Entity_Representation.md\|DragAnything]], [[analysis/CVPR_2025/MotionPro_A_Precise_Motion_Controller_for_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionPro]], [[analysis/arxiv_2023/AnimateAnything_Fine_Grained_Open_Domain_Image_Animation_with_Motion_Guidance.md\|AnimateAnything]] | 不等于 camera extrinsics generation，不能直接算 sketch2camera |
| sketch-to-multiview / sketch-to-3D | single freehand sketch + caption -> multi-view images | 可借鉴 camera-aware view generation，但不是连续 camera movement |

当前可以据此提出一个清晰 research gap：**把导演在屏幕上画的 2D 箭头/曲线/构图草图，自动 disambiguate 成带 look-at、depth、speed、focal schedule 和 collision/framing constraints 的 6-DoF camera trajectory**。这一任务目前没有类似 HumanML3D 的成熟公开标准数据集。

## 8. 对研究选题的直接建议

### 8.1 如果目标是“文本/分镜 -> camera movement”

最稳路线是：

1. 用 [[analysis/ECCV_2024/E_T_the_Exceptional_Trajectories_Text_to_camera_trajectory_generation_with_character_awareness.md\|E.T.]] / DataDoP / PulpMotion / Storytelling Animations 建 planner 数据和评价。
2. 输出统一 camera trajectory 表示：SE(3) + intrinsics + look-at anchor + focal schedule。
3. 用 [[analysis/ICLR_2025/CameraCtrl_Enabling_Camera_Control_for_Text_to_Video_Generation.md\|CameraCtrl]] / [[analysis/CVPR_2025/GEN3C_3D_Informed_World_Consistent_Video_Generation_with_Precise_Camera_Control.md\|GEN3C]] / [[analysis/NEURIPS_2024/Director3D_Real_world_Camera_Trajectory_and_3D_Scene_Generation_from_Text.md\|Director3D]]-I2V / [[analysis/ICCV_2025/ReCamMaster_Camera_Controlled_Generative_Rendering_from_A_Single_Video.md\|ReCamMaster]] 作为 actuator。
4. 引入 verifier：framing、subject visibility、collision、jerk、semantic shot primitive。

### 8.2 如果目标是“sketch2camera movement”

这是最有空缺的方向。可定义任务为：

```text
first frame / RGBD / rough 3D scene
+ 2D screen stroke / storyboard arrows / desired framing boxes
+ optional text
-> physically plausible 6-DoF camera trajectory
-> camera-controlled generated or re-rendered video
```

关键技术点：

- 2D stroke 到 3D path 的深度消歧：floor plane、target anchor、look-at point、spline control points。
- 将草图拆成 camera path、framing target、speed profile、focal length schedule。
- 用 on-screen framing loss 约束人物/物体保持在画面中。
- 用 [[analysis/arxiv_2024/ChatCam_Empowering_Camera_Control_through_Conversational_AI.md\|ChatCam]]-I2V / [[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2025/MotionCanvas_Cinematic_Shot_Design_with_Controllable_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCanvas]] / [[analysis/arxiv_2025/Free_Form_Motion_Control_A_Synthetic_Video_Generation_Dataset_with_Controllable_Camera_and_Object_Motions.md\|FMC]] 做执行器，用 E.T./DataDoP/PulpMotion 做语言和摄影先验。

### 8.3 如果目标是“人体动作生成 + camera”

优先读：

- `gpt.md`：角色-aware camera trajectory。
- `gpt.md`：framing-aware joint human-camera generation。
- `gemini.md`：joint character-camera motion synthesis。
- `gemini.md`：4D human motion + text 的 viewpoint planning。

一个合理的模型结构是 `human motion latent + camera trajectory latent + screen framing latent` 三路联合建模，而不是简单拼接 SMPL-X 和 camera SE(3)。[[analysis/ICLR_2026/Pulp_Motion_Framing_aware_multimodal_camera_and_human_motion_generation.md\|Pulp Motion]] 的 on-screen framing auxiliary modality 是这里最值得借鉴的中间表征。

## 9. 最终合并结论

两份 LLM 稿件合并后，应形成以下稳定判断：

1. **camera movement generation 是真实且正在成形的方向**，但名称分散在 virtual cinematography、camera trajectory generation、camera-controlled video、viewpoint planning、re-camera/NVS 中。
2. **Text-to-camera 主线已经有代表作**：[[analysis/CGF_2024/Cinematographic_Camera_Diffusion_Model.md\|CCD]]、[[analysis/ECCV_2024/E_T_the_Exceptional_Trajectories_Text_to_camera_trajectory_generation_with_character_awareness.md\|E.T.]]、[[analysis/NEURIPS_2024/Director3D_Real_world_Camera_Trajectory_and_3D_Scene_Generation_from_Text.md\|Director3D]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2024/ChatCam_Empowering_Camera_Control_through_Conversational_AI.md\|ChatCam]]、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/GenDoP_Auto_regressive_Camera_Trajectory_Generation_as_a_Director_of_Photography.md\|GenDoP]]。
3. **2026 最值得关注的是 human-camera joint generation**：[[analysis/ICLR_2026/Pulp_Motion_Framing_aware_multimodal_camera_and_human_motion_generation.md\|Pulp Motion]]、[[analysis/CVPR_2026/Towards_Storytelling_Animations_Joint_Synthesis_of_Human_and_Camera_Motions.md\|Towards Storytelling Animations]]、[[analysis/ICLR_2026/AdaViewPlanner_Adapting_Video_Diffusion_Models_for_Viewpoint_Planning_in_4D_Scenes.md\|AdaViewPlanner]]。
4. **Sketch2camera 仍是空缺**：现代工作多是“用户给定 3D path 后执行”，不是“草图自动生成 6-DoF camera path”。
5. **Controller 生态已经很强**：[[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2024/MotionCtrl_A_Unified_and_Flexible_Motion_Controller_for_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCtrl]]、[[analysis/ICLR_2025/CameraCtrl_Enabling_Camera_Control_for_Text_to_Video_Generation.md\|CameraCtrl]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2025/Free_Form_Motion_Control_A_Synthetic_Video_Generation_Dataset_with_Controllable_Camera_and_Object_Motions.md\|FMC]]、[[analysis/SIGGRAPH_2025/MotionCanvas_Cinematic_Shot_Design_with_Controllable_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionCanvas]]、[[analysis/CVPR_2025/GEN3C_3D_Informed_World_Consistent_Video_Generation_with_Precise_Camera_Control.md\|GEN3C]]、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/GenDoP_Auto_regressive_Camera_Trajectory_Generation_as_a_Director_of_Photography.md\|GenDoP / DataDoP]]-I2V、[[analysis/ICCV_2025/ReCamMaster_Camera_Controlled_Generative_Rendering_from_A_Single_Video.md\|ReCamMaster]] 等可以作为下游执行器。
6. **LLM 容易错分的项目必须降级**：SketchVideo、[[analysis/CVPR_2025/MotionPro_A_Precise_Motion_Controller_for_Image_to_Video_Generation.md\|MotionPro]]、[[analysis/arxiv_2023/AnimateAnything_Fine_Grained_Open_Domain_Image_Animation_with_Motion_Guidance.md\|AnimateAnything]]、AKiRa、CameraBench、RealEstate10K、DL3DV、MVImgNet 不应放进主线 camera movement generation 表。
