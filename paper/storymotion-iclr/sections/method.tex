\section{Method}

\subsection{Human-owned relation-aware representation}

Stage~1 represents Human motion, an explicit Human--Camera interaction channel, and Camera
motion with Human-owned, interaction, and Camera latent channels. The Human decoder consumes
only the Human-owned channel; Camera and framing decoding additionally consume the interaction
and Camera channels. This is a structural information-flow statement, not a semantic
disentanglement claim.

\subsection{Protected asymmetric generation}

Stage~2 freezes the finalized Human generator. The Camera flow consumes Camera text together
with fixed observed or generated Human context. Camera optimization excludes Human parameters,
and every StoryMotion tokenizer and generator boundary is non-causal.

\subsection{Two-pass inference}

Human generation and Camera generation use separate passes. The Camera pass begins only after
the Human sample is complete and fixed. Joint-parallel and evolving-Human solvers are outside
the reported method.

\subsection{Training and artifact contract}

Each result binds a Stage~1 checkpoint and owning decoder, train-only normalization statistics,
train/eval caches and ordered sample identities, seed, batch sizes, sampler, code revision,
hardware, and checkpoint state. A comparison row may not combine fields from different
endpoints.
