\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Human motion generation.}
Discuss text-conditioned Human motion generation and identify the frozen Human generator used
by \storymotion{} without presenting Camera results as Human evidence.

\paragraph{Camera trajectory and cinematographic control.}
Separate Camera generation conditioned on an observed actor trajectory from systems that also
own Human generation. Keep downstream video-generation controllers outside the claimed task.

\paragraph{Joint Human--Camera generation.}
Compare changed design slots---Human ownership, factorization, representation, interaction
direction, and sampling schedule---against Pulp Motion and synchronous joint-generation work.
Do not turn matched cohort membership into a single-variable architecture comparison when
representation, decoder, mode, or runtime differs.

\paragraph{Conditional composition.}
Position two-pass generated-Human Camera inference as an audited system interface. Report the
MotionLab-to-C0 and MoMask-to-C0 zero-retraining pairs as system-level comparisons: both are
executable, but their changed Human generator, representation, and decoder prevent a
single-variable architecture claim.
