\section{Limitations}

The reported system is offline, non-causal, and two-pass. It is evaluated on one data domain and
one Human backbone, so we do not claim model-agnostic transfer. Generated-Human Camera inference
inherits Human errors and condition shift; factual Camera geometry is only a dataset-target
diagnostic in this setting. Raw Camera captions limit coordinate semantics and compositional
control, while current Camera-text ownership remains evidence pending. Symmetric controls form a
field-wise mixed Pareto rather than proving global asymmetric superiority. Native baselines may
change representation, decoder, normalization, generation mode, and runtime simultaneously.
The method does not provide arbitrary editing, actor--director co-design, transferable Camera
programs, downstream video-generation control, streaming, or real-time inference.

Phase-C Stage~1 provenance is a separate causal question from the finalized Stage~2 frozen-Human
guarantee. The paper will report the Phase-C protection result only after the preregistered
formal reconstruction and downstream gates close.
