correct, fixed-point-free shuffled, contradictory, and absent Camera text. The matched family

\subsection{Simple Human-to-Camera composition}

Audit representation and coordinate compatibility before composing models. We evaluate
MotionLab-to-C0 and MoMask-to-C0 by decoding each Human output to Pulp Human199 and re-encoding it
with the exact StoryMotion Human encoder before the same Camera model consumes Camera text. Both
pairs are zero-retraining system comparisons and regress semantic, framing, and Human evidence;
no adapter is required or authorized.
