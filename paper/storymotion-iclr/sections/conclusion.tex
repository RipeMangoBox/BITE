\section{Conclusion}

\storymotion{} studies a bounded capability-preservation problem: adding Camera generation to a
finalized Human generator without allowing Camera training to rewrite the Human pathway. Its
directed factorization exposes Human generation, observed-Human Camera generation, and
generated-Human two-pass generation as distinct evaluation interfaces. The final conclusion
will retain field-wise trade-offs and will promote Camera-text, visual, cascade, or resource
claims only when their preregistered evidence gates are formally closed.
